Before you start

# one name, one look, every time.

This book is the single source of truth for how 3not8 Studios looks, sounds and shows up. Anything that carries our name follows it: a reel end card, a quotation, a crew tee, a hoarding on Kankanady road.

Consistency is not decoration. Every time a client sees the same orange, the same green and the same marks, the brand gets cheaper to recognise and harder to forget. Every off brand post undoes some of that work.

If something is not covered here, do not improvise. Ask the brand owner, then we add the answer to the next version of this book.

The idea

# the other half.

A 3 is half of an 8. Put a mirror against it and the 8 appears.

Every client walks in with half of something: an idea, a launch, a story they cannot quite tell yet. That is their vision. We bring the other half: the execution. Put the two together and it is whole.

That is why the signature line is **Your vision. Our execution.** It is why our layouts split in two, why photographs get cut by half circles, and why the 8 in our mark is never shown complete.

**The one rule**

We never explain this in public. Not in captions, ads, pitches or on the website. People discover it, and the ones who do will tell others. Explaining it kills it.

**Say it**Three Not Eight

**Write it**3not8 Studios. One word, lowercase not, capital S on Studios.

**Never**3NOT8, 3 Not 8, Three not Eight Studios, 308 Studios in running text

Writing rules

- No dashes in any copy. Use commas and full stops.
- No promises we cannot control: views, virality, followers.
- The word not in our name is never paired with another negative in a tagline.
- Headlines in lowercase, body in sentence case. No all caps paragraphs.
- English first. Kannada, Tulu or Konkani lines only when written by a native speaker.

Glow

# light, in pixels.

Our only gradient. It reads like a light source seen through a camera sensor, and it belongs to backgrounds, loaders, reel transitions and presentation covers.

- Dark mode: Ember to Kerria Gold glowing out of Deep Forest.
- Light mode: Signal Orange to Gold on Paper.
- Never under body text. Never as a logo fill.
- No other gradients, ever.

Headline typeface

# Grityle.

Bold, geometric and full of character. Grityle is for headlines, campaign lines and anything set large. Its quirks (the hooked g, the round counters) echo the curves of our mark.

- Set headlines in lowercase.
- Leading 95 to 100 percent. Tracking minus 1 percent.
- Never below 28 px on screen or 18 pt in print.
- Never for body copy or long lists.

**Licence**

Grityle is a commercial font. Buy the desktop and web licence in the company's name before launch. Until it is licensed, use Montserrat Black for headlines, as this book does.

Pattern

# born from the t.

The curl on the t of our wordmark, mirrored and repeated, becomes the main brand pattern. It runs vertically, like film through a gate.

- Two colours only: orange on Deep Forest, or Deep Forest on orange.
- Use as a full background, a border band or a bleed off one edge.
- Never behind body text or the logo.
- Never rotate, recolour or add a third colour.

05 Graphic language3not8 Studios Brand Guidelines v1.0

31

The split

# two halves, one frame.

Our signature layout device. A photograph is cut by half circles and arcs taken from the 3 and the 8, so the image is always partly revealed, partly withheld.

- Pair it with the two part line: Your vision. Our execution.
- Vision sits top or left, execution bottom or right.
- Shapes are Deep Forest, Night or Paper. Never orange over a photograph.
- One split per layout.

Launch checklist

# before the world sees it.

**Approvals**

Anything new that carries the logo, from a sticker to a hoarding, is approved by the brand owner before it is printed, posted or sent. Reuse of an approved template does not need fresh approval.

**Trademark**IP India search and filing for 3not8 (word and logo) in classes 35, 41 and 42. Nothing large gets printed until the search is clean.

**Domain and handles**Secure the domain, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and WhatsApp Business names together, before announcing.

**Legal line**Update GST trade name, bank account name and agreements, or print the legal entity name next to 3not8 Studios on invoices until re registration.

**Font licence**Buy the Grityle desktop and web licence in the company name.

**Retire Pixora Clips**Replace profile photos, bios, signatures, letterheads, templates, watermarks and Google Business Profile on one day.

**Print proofs**Proof business cards and signage against a Pantone guide before the first run.
