The brief

They wanted the race to look the way it feels from the fence. Loud, close and a little out of control.

We walked the track the day before and marked five places where the action comes to the camera. Low angles, long lenses and fast shutters, so every drop of water holds its shape.

Selects went to the organisers the same evening. The full set and the vertical cuts followed within the week, graded as one series.
